# Asset-prompter för piratkartan

Klistra in i ChatGPT (DALL-E) eller annan bildgenerator. Skrivna på engelska — DALL-E hanterar det bättre.

**Stilen vi söker:** en *amatörmässig 1700-talskartograf* — någon som har quill-penna, bläck och akvarell, jobbar på pergament, men som inte är så duktig på att rita. Resultatet ska kännas autentiskt för perioden (sepia, urblekta toner, handkolorerat) men med vingliga linjer, fel proportioner och färg utanför konturerna. **Inte** ett modernt barn med kritor. **Inte** ett polerat 1800-tals stick.

Gemensamt: be om **transparent bakgrund** (PNG) och **isolated subject, no scene around it**.

---

## Gemensamt stilfragment

> A naïve, awkward hand-drawn illustration in iron-gall ink and faded watercolour wash on aged parchment, in the style of an enthusiastic but genuinely unskilled 17th–18th century amateur cartographer. Quill-pen linework with wobbly hesitant strokes and uneven pressure, the proportions clearly wrong, watercolour wash spilling outside the outlines. Period palette only — sepia, warm browns, muted rust red, faded teal, ochre, muted green — all slightly aged and oxidised. The illustration looks like an authentic awkward sketch from the age of sail, made by someone who has heard about the subject but isn't very good at depicting it. NOT a modern drawing, NOT a child's crayon doodle, NOT clean digital art, NOT a polished engraving. Transparent background, isolated subject only.

## Om resultatet är fel åt något håll

**Om för polerat (för fint, för proffsigt):** lägg till
- "wobbly hesitant lines, visible quill pressure changes"
- "watercolour wash spills outside the outlines"
- "lopsided, asymmetric proportions"
- "the cartographer is clearly unskilled — earnest but bad at drawing"
- "looks like an authentic awkward sketch by a 17th-century amateur, NOT a polished engraving"

**Om för modernt-barnsligt (kritor, filtpenna, dagens papper):** lägg till
- "quill pen and iron-gall ink, NOT crayons, NOT felt-tip, NOT modern materials"
- "aged parchment paper, NOT modern lined notebook"
- "period palette only — sepia, muted earth tones — NO bright modern colours"
- "17th–18th century materials and aesthetic, just executed clumsily"

**Negativa cues att alltid ha med:**
- "no clean digital vector lines, no modern illustration style, no neon, no photorealism, no professional polish, no children's storybook style, no crayon, no felt-tip"
